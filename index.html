FAQ

Questions people ask first

Who writes the answers?

AstroOracle's AI astrologers. Your chart is calculated by our own Jyotish engine, and the astrologer you picked reads it and writes the answer in your language. Ask one directly whether it is an AI and it will tell you.

I don't know my exact birth time. Can I still use it?

Yes. The reading is then cast from your Moon sign, houses are counted from there, and every answer says so instead of quietly guessing.

Which system of astrology is this?

Vedic (Jyotish): the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsha, whole-sign houses, Vimshottari dasha, divisional charts D9 and D10, and real daily transits.

Will it predict my death, my illness or a baby's sex?

No. It will not predict death or lifespan, will not diagnose or prescribe, and never predicts the sex of an unborn child. For symptoms, please see a doctor.

What happens to my birth details?

They are stored to calculate your chart and answer your questions, and nothing more. See the privacy policy.

Is an answer ever wrong?

It can be. Every answer has thumbs up, thumbs down and a report button, and those reports come straight to us and shape what comes next.
