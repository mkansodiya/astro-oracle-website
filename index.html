FAQ

Common questions, clear answers.

Is AstroOracle free to use?

Every account starts with three free questions. After that, question packs keep you going — no subscription, and an answer that fails is never counted.

How accurate are the predictions?

The calculations are exact: sidereal positions with the Lahiri ayanamsha, your dasha to the second and today's real transits. The reading built on them is interpretation, so treat it as guidance, not certainty.

Can I ask questions in Hindi?

Yes — English, Hindi or Hinglish. The answer comes back in the language you wrote in.

Do I need my exact birth time?

No. Without it the reading is cast from your Moon sign, houses are counted from there, and the answer says so instead of quietly guessing.

Will my personal data be safe?

Your birth details are used to calculate your chart and answer your questions, nothing else — no selling, no advertising. See the privacy policy.

Who actually writes the answers?

AstroOracle's AI astrologers. Your chart is calculated by our own Jyotish engine, and the astrologer you picked reads it and writes the answer. Ask one directly whether it is an AI and it will tell you.
