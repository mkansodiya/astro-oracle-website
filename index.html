Questions, answered plainly.

Who writes the answers?

AstroOracle's AI astrologers. Our own Jyotish engine calculates your chart; the astrologer you pick reads it and writes the answer in your language.

Is it free?

Your Kundli, Kundli matching, the daily card, the planner, the upay practices and the lessons are free, always. Every account also starts with three free questions; after that a question pack keeps you going. There is no subscription, and an answer that fails is never counted.

Can I use it in Hindi?

Yes. Every screen is in English and Hindi, and you can switch at any time. Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish and the answer comes back in the language you wrote in.

I don't know my exact birth time.

Then the reading is cast from your Moon sign, houses are counted from there, and the answer says so instead of pretending to a precision it doesn't have.

Which system of astrology is this?

Vedic (Jyotish): the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsha, whole-sign houses, Vimshottari dasha, D9 and D10 divisional charts, and real daily transits.

What does a match code share?

A first name, Moon sign, nakshatra (with its pada) and Manglik status. Never a birth date, time or place.

What happens to my birth details?

They are used to calculate your chart and answer your questions, and nothing else: no selling, no advertising. See the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. AstroOracle is on Android, from Google Play.
